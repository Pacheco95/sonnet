## Verdict

The target is met on this card. In all four scenes the 10,000-draw, 100-light frame at 1080p takes 2.8 to 4.5 ms of GPU time (2.9 to 4.5 ms of wall clock), at most 27 percent of the 16.6 ms budget, with TAA on. The RTX 2050 is at or below a 2020-era mid-range desktop card, so the target holds on the hardware it names with a margin of about 3.7 times in the worst scene. The `forward` pass dominates the open scenes (2.0 ms, about 47 percent of the frame), followed by the four shadow cascades together (about 1.1 ms) and TAA (0.38 ms). Occlusion culling pays off only behind the wall (2.84 ms against 3.15 ms); in the open scene it costs 0.2 ms for the depth pyramid and saves nothing, since nothing is hidden. A second view still fits (8.8 ms at 10,000 draws), but two views of the heaviest scene use more than half of the budget. These are Release numbers from a synthetic scene on one laptop, with the clock below its maximum, so a sustained thermal run could differ.
